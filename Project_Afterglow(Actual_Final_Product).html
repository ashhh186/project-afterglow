Chronology

Sixty years of leaving things.

1958

Vanguard 1 enters orbit; it is still there today.

1967–69

Surveyor 3 lands, Apollo 11 follows, and Apollo 10’s lunar module “Snoopy” is sent into solar orbit.

1971–72

Three lunar rovers and several ALSEP science stations are left on the Moon.

1972–77

Pioneer 10 & 11 and Voyager 1 & 2 launch carrying plaques and Golden Records toward the stars.

1997–2008

Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, and Phoenix explore Mars.

2012–24

Both Voyagers enter interstellar space; InSight finishes, Perseverance lays a sample depot, and Ingenuity completes 72 flights.
